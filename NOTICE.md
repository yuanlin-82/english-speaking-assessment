# NOTICE

This repository documents **methodology and scoring contracts** for English speaking assessment in interview / talent products.

It does **not** include:

- production prompts, judge templates, or ASR/TTS configs
- proprietary speech-vendor APIs, keys, or raw vendor score schemas
- copyrighted lexicon dumps (e.g. published learner dictionaries)
- candidate audio, transcripts with PII, or labeled scoring corpora
- production weight tables, IELTS-style mapping tables, or report templates as shipped

Task wording and score examples here are **illustrative**. Do not treat them as the live product specification.
