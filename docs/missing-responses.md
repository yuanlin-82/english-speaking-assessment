# Missing responses, retries, and what the report shows

Field / product note on **coverage**, not production formulas.

---

## What counts as unanswered

If there is **no valid recording** for an item (including cases with video but no usable audio / ASR, or the candidate never produced scorable speech), it is treated as **unanswered** for composition.

No fluency or pronunciation score is invented from silence or dead air.

---

## Interruption and retry

When the session drops for environmental reasons (for example network issues), the candidate may **re-enter within a time window** and **retake that item**.

If two takes exist for the same item: **both are scored; the higher enters the composite**. Exact timers and UX copy stay private.

Skip / never speak remains unanswered.

---

## How missingness enters the composite

Unanswered items **lower the assembled total** through an internal missingness rule. Exact coefficients / exponents are **not** published here and are **not** printed on the candidate-facing report.

If HR asks, the rule can be explained. In practice few ask.

---

## What the report shows

The report presents a **composite** (and dimension scores). It does **not** show a separate “battery incomplete” / “answered N of M” label on the face of the report.

---

## Operations around incomplete attempts

- Candidates who hit problems and **contact HR** often get a **retake / makeup**.
- Candidates who **do not contact anyone** or **abandon mid-way** are often treated as **out of process** (screened out).

---

## Takeaways

1. No valid recording → unanswered.  
2. Eligible retry → score both takes, keep the higher.  
3. Missingness lowers the composite; the report does not separately label incompleteness.  
4. Deduction formula stays private.
